01

If your infrastructure strategy depends on API lock-in, your competitive moat has a 6-month expiration date. Start modeling how your economics change when inference happens on customer hardware, not your servers.

OpenAI agreed to remove unspecified safety constraints under Pentagon pressure. Anthropic claimed it refused similar demands. Stop evaluating AI companies on stated principles. Evaluate them on who funds them and what they've actually shipped. No lab will remain independent long enough for principle to matter against Pentagon capital.

Expect smaller labs to cave within quarters, not years.
