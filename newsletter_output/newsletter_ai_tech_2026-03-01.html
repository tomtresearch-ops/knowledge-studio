01

The Bigger Picture
The "winner takes all" narrative is officially dead. The market is solidifying into three non-overlapping layers: state-aligned labs (OpenAI, potentially others) winning government and premium enterprise; open-source models (Qwen, Mistral) winning cost-sensitive production deployments; and ideological positioning (Anthropic's refusal) becoming a brand asset. None of these layers compete for the same customer. This is what a mature market looks like—specialization by value, not consolidation by capability. The real test arrives in 60 days when Anthropic's court challenge gets resolved. If the company survives, it proves that principled refusal is economically viable. If it collapses, every lab learns that governance positions are a luxury good, only affordable in bull markets.
