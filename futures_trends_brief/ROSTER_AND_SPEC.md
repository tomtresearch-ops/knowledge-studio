## BRIEF SPECIFICATION

### Cadence
Twice weekly: **Tuesday and Friday**

### Angle
Big picture futurism -- where the world is heading across technology, geopolitics, economics, demographics, culture, and science. Pattern recognition across long time horizons, not daily news reaction. Think analyst/futurist, not journalist.

### Audience
Generalist strategic thinkers, macro-oriented, interested in where things are heading over **1-10 year horizons**. Not specialists in any one domain.

### Structure
Same as other briefs:
- **Top Signal** -- 1-2 things you absolutely need to know
- **Key Developments** -- 3-5 items with context
- **Emerging Patterns** -- THE CENTERPIECE. Connecting dots across domains that vertical-specific briefs cannot see.
- **Worth Your Time** -- 1-2 deep reads/watches to queue up
- **Signal Health** -- meta-assessment of source quality

### Key Instruction (Tom's exact words)
> "This brief synthesizes across domains, not within one. When the AI brief identifies agentic labor displacement and the health brief identifies longevity timeline shifts, this brief connects them -- 'workforce planning models break when retirement assumptions change.' That cross-domain synthesis is the unique value."

> "The emerging patterns section is the centerpiece here. This brief lives and dies on connecting dots across domains that the vertical-specific briefs can't see."

### Architecture
- Operates at the **meta/macro level**
- **Ingests the other briefs as source material** (unique to this vertical)
- Does NOT duplicate the pipeline structure -- uses same architecture, different sources and topic framing
- Accumulation model with threshold-based publishing (daily scan, brief when signal warrants)
