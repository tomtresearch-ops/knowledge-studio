# 2026 FUTURES REPORT — Working Plan
*"The What and The When"*

## Vision
A forward-looking futures report, published January/February 2026. Not a year-in-review — a rigorous, domain-by-domain analysis of what's coming and on what timeline. Brief "state of play" context per domain, then deep on predictions with timeframes. Tom's 25+ year focus on timing/prediction at the heart, AI aggregation and synthesis supporting.

This becomes the template for quarterly updates and informs the twice-weekly brief's framing.

## Output Formats (planned)
- Long-form written report (the master document)
- Slideshow / presentation deck
- Big-time video production
- Template reusable for quarterly and future annual reports

## Core Philosophy
- **Forward-looking**: What's coming and WHEN
- **Prediction-oriented**: Take positions, assign timeframes
- **Cross-domain synthesis**: Where domains collide is where the insight is
- **Opinionated**: Tom's perspective, not consensus averaging
- **Meant to get attention**: This is for publishing and sharing

## The Three Layers (What Makes This Report Different)

1. **The Aggregation** — what institutions and key voices are saying, the data, the state of play. The foundation. Nobody has time to read ARK + McKinsey + Gartner + 10 futurists. We did.
2. **Tom's Synthesis Within Each Domain** — the opinionated prediction layer. Not consensus-averaging. Informed by all of it, but taking positions. This appears at the end of each domain section.
3. **Cross-Domain Synthesis** — where domains collide. The stuff nobody else writes because they stay in their lane. This comes at the end of the report after the reader has all the domain context. This is the centerpiece.

Layer 1 is what McKinsey does. Layer 2 is what individual futurists do. Layer 3 is what nobody does well — it requires someone who's been watching ALL of these domains for decades. That's the differentiation.

## Report Structure Per Domain
Each domain follows this pattern:
1. **State of Play** (brief — just enough to orient the reader)
2. **What's Coming** (aggregated predictions — what the institutions and voices are saying)
3. **The Timeline** (when — near-term 2026-2027, mid-term 2028-2030, horizon 2030+)
4. **Key Voices** (where do the experts agree/disagree — the tension points)
5. **Wild Cards** (what could accelerate or derail the timeline)
6. **Tom's Take** (the opinionated synthesis — this is where the report's voice lives)

## Domains

### 1. AI & Machine Intelligence (heaviest coverage)
- Agents, autonomy, reasoning — the capability trajectory
- Enterprise adoption and labor displacement curves
- AI governance and safety
- Where we are on the S-curve and what the next inflection looks like
- **The When**: AGI timelines, autonomous agents in production, white-collar restructuring waves

### 2. Robotics & Physical AI
- Humanoid robotics (Optimus, Figure, 1X, etc.)
- Manufacturing/warehouse/logistics automation
- Autonomous vehicles at scale
- Convergence: when AI intelligence meets physical capability
- **The When**: Physical labor disruption follows cognitive by how many years?

### 3. Longevity & Future of Medicine
- Aging biology: rapamycin, senolytics, mTOR pathway
- Genetic screening, polygenic risk, personalized intervention
- GLP-1 revolution and metabolic health
- BCI and neurotechnology
- **The When**: Life expectancy inflection points, when do interventions go mainstream vs. elite-only

### 4. Energy & Climate
- Solar/battery/grid storage trajectory (China's dominance)
- Nuclear fusion timeline (Commonwealth, Helion, Gates-backed)
- Geothermal for AI compute
- Energy abundance vs. energy sovereignty
- **The When**: Grid parity milestones, when does fossil fuel become economically irrational

### 5. Geopolitics & Demographics
- US-China technology competition
- Demographic decline curves (developed world) vs. growth (Africa/South Asia)
- Deglobalization and supply chain restructuring
- AI as geopolitical power multiplier
- **The When**: Demographic inflection points, trade regime shifts, power rebalancing

### 6. Economics & Work
- Knowledge worker displacement at scale
- New economic models — UBI, post-scarcity thinking, wealth concentration
- The retirement crisis (longevity + displacement pincer)
- Access stratification (who gets the future first)
- **The When**: Unemployment inflection, policy response windows, new models emerging

### 7. Space & Frontier Tech
- SpaceX/Starship, orbital infrastructure buildout
- Space-based compute thesis (real or bubble?)
- Quantum computing: where it actually matters
- BCI commercialization
- **The When**: Commercialization milestones, what's real vs. hype on what timeline

### 8. Culture, Identity & Society
- AI's impact on creativity, media, and truth
- Platform decay and the information ecosystem collapse
- Education transformation
- How society adapts (or doesn't) to exponential change
- **The When**: Social adaptation curves — when does the public catch up

## Cross-Domain Synthesis (THE CENTERPIECE)
This is where the report earns its keep. Where domains collide:
- **Longevity + AI labor** → retirement as an institution breaks
- **Energy abundance + AI compute demand** → who controls the surplus
- **Demographic decline + automation** → different countries face this differently
- **Genetic stratification + wealth gaps** → who gets to live longer
- **AI capability + governance lag** → the window of maximum disruption
- **Physical AI + energy** → when robots are cheap AND power is cheap, everything changes

This is where Tom's perspective and pattern recognition over 25 years drives the narrative.

## Research Approach
1. Collect forward-looking reports from institutional voices (McKinsey, Gartner, WEF, a16z Big Ideas, ARK Invest, etc.)
2. Aggregate predictions and timelines from key futurists in the roster
3. Pull current state-of-play data for each domain
4. Tom provides perspective, framing, timing predictions, and the connective tissue
5. AI synthesizes, structures, and drafts
6. Iterate with Tom on each section
