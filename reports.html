Creatine Protocol

Compiled Jun 20, 2026 · KS library (Candow/DOAC, Rhonda Patrick FoundMyFitness, DeLauer, Kelley MD, Azadi, Tim Ferriss) + web research

Best Kind

Creatine monohydrate. Full stop. 30+ years of human research. Cheapest. Most studied. A 2025 RCT confirmed monohydrate and HCl produce identical strength and body composition results in elite athletes. HCl has higher water solubility but no meaningful absorption advantage — the "38x more soluble" claim doesn't translate to better outcomes. Only reason to consider HCl: GI distress with monohydrate, which affects a small minority.

Look for: pure creatine monohydrate, Creapure-certified if you want German-sourced pharmaceutical grade (DeLauer's preferred).

How Much

GoalDoseSource
Muscle / performance / baseline3–5g/dayCandow (120+ papers), ISSN, Azadi
Bone protection (40+)8–12g/dayDr. Darren Candow specifically
Brain / cognitive enhancement10g/dayRhonda Patrick — 10g doubles brain creatine vs 2–4g (neuroimaging confirmed)
Sleep deprivation / acute stress20–25g that dayRhonda Patrick travel protocol; 2022 Scientific Reports study
Rhonda Patrick's actual protocol: 10g daily baseline, bumps to 20–25g when sleep-deprived or traveling. Most evidence-informed stack right now for someone optimizing both body and brain.

Loading phase: Optional. 20g/day × 5–7 days saturates faster. Skip it and take 5g daily — same saturation point in ~3–4 weeks. No performance difference at steady state.

When

Consistency > timing. That's the consistent finding across all sources.

  • On training days: Post-workout slight edge — muscle blood flow is higher, nutrient uptake enhanced.
  • On rest days: Morning with food. Some evidence carbs improve uptake.
  • If taking 10g+: Split doses — 5g AM / 5g PM. Rhonda Patrick's approach. Better GI tolerance, more consistent absorption.
  • Timing note: Creatine absorbs in 1–2 hours, peaks up to 4 hours. For a pre-workout cognitive edge, take 60–90 min before.

Key Signals from Your Library

  • DeLauer [KS 731]: Frames creatine as the underrated longevity compound — cellular energy buffer against mitochondrial decline. Older adults see proportionally bigger benefits than young athletes because cognitive aging is fundamentally an energy problem.
  • Dr. Kelley, ER doc [KS 928]: Flag it to your doctor if taking 10g+. Creatine's metabolite (creatinine) elevates in bloodwork and can falsely suggest kidney damage, triggering unnecessary workups. Kidneys are fine — the number isn't.
  • Alzheimer's pilot (2025): 20g/day × 8 weeks → +11% brain creatine, +17% working memory. Early but significant signal.
  • Vegans / vegetarians: Dramatically larger response — lower dietary baseline means higher ceiling for supplementation benefit.

Bottom Line

Creatine monohydrate, 5g minimum daily, 10g if brain function matters to you. Post-workout or morning, split if 10g+. Don't overthink timing. Consistency is the whole game — it saturates over weeks, not hours.

The Race to the Personal "Jarvis" On-Ramp — 2026 Landscape

Research memo · Jun 23, 2026 · web research + Tech Timelines · INTERNAL

Editor's note (Tom, 2026-06-23) — read the white space through this lens: Jarvis's power is what it does for the living — the ultimate personal assistant. Digital Estate serves the dead's affairs, and even that (wrapping up an estate, a memorial avatar) ultimately benefits the living. So the estate/executor angle below is a go-to-market beachhead — urgency, willingness to pay, a fragmented market no incumbent touches, and a cascade into the whole household — not the core of the prize. The household cascade is the bridge: estate entry → living family already inside the product → living-person skins → Jarvis. Estate is the door; the living-person assistant is the house. Don't mistake the wedge for the value.

1. Thesis

A personal agent is only as good as the data it's grounded in. The on-ramp leader is therefore whoever already custodies the most of your life — email, calendar, drive, docs, photos, accounts. Custody, not model quality, is the moat. The on-ramp matures in three steps: passive ingestion (apps that already hold your data become the foundation) → active interrogation (the agent asks a million questions, gets granted accounts/subscriptions/documents) → composed picture (a working model of the person). Leadership differs by cohort and there is real white space for a neutral cross-custodian aggregator.

3. By Cohort — who is the natural on-ramp

Cohort Natural on-ramp Why
Knowledge workers Google or Microsoft (whichever owns their work identity) Email + calendar + drive already there; agent ships inside the same account. Microsoft if employer-bound; Google if Workspace-native.
Writers / creators (large body of original long-form scattered across drive, email, local files, publishing platforms) No clean winner — the gap. Closest: OpenAI/Claude via connectors (vendor-neutral pull) or Notion (if already self-organized there) Their corpus is fragmented by definition — Drive + local + email + Substack/YT/CMS. No single custodian holds it, so connector-based composition beats custody. This is the white-space cohort.
Executives Microsoft (corporate) backed by an EA workflow Identity, scheduling, comms all in 365; governance + delegation matter more than consumer features.
Everyday consumers Apple (iPhone-centric) or Google (Android-centric); Amazon in the home Whoever owns the phone OS owns the passive-ingestion layer. Apple wins intimacy if/when Siri ships; Google wins capability now.

4. Mechanism / Maturity Curve

Passive ingestion → Active interrogation → Composed picture.

Stage What it is Who's there today Enabling tech
1. Passive ingestion App already stores/gleans your data; becomes the foundation with no extra work Google (Workspace Intelligence, default-on), Microsoft (Graph), Apple (on-device index) Native custody; identity graph; on-device indexing
2. Active interrogation Agent asks questions, is granted access to documents/subscriptions/accounts beyond the home turf OpenAI & Anthropic (OAuth connectors), Microsoft (federated connectors), Gemini Spark (cross-app goals) MCP (open standard, 485+ Claude integrations), OAuth connectors, Copilot connector graph
3. Composed picture A persistent working model of the person; proactive, cross-source OpenAI (cross-chat Memory + Pulse/scheduled briefings), Gemini Spark (autonomous background goals), Notion (workspace-as-memory) Long-term memory primitives, proactive scheduling, agent runtimes

Read: Custody players (Google/MS/Apple) own Stage 1 but are gated to their own data. Model players (OpenAI/Anthropic) leapfrog to Stage 2–3 via connectors but own none of the data and can be cut off. Nobody yet owns a neutral Stage-3 composed picture spanning all custodians.

5. White Space — the independent "Jarvis on-ramp" opening

What no incumbent will build because it cannibalizes their lock-in:

  • Neutrality / cross-custodian composition. Every leader composes only their slice (Google won't ingest your Apple Health; Apple won't read your Workspace). A neutral aggregator that legitimately holds OAuth to all custodians + local files + publishing platforms owns the only complete composed picture. This is precisely the writer/creator gap.
  • Portability as a product. Incumbents optimize for lock-in; neutral formats / model routers / exportable memory are an explicitly anti-incumbent feature. A user-owned, portable "life graph" is structurally something only an independent can sell.
  • The estate / executor wedge. Digital inheritance is fragmented and broken in 2026: RUFADAA gives only partial fiduciary access, platforms default to ToS, no cross-platform death-verification standard, legacy tools have low adoption. An aggregator that already custodies cross-account access for the living user is the natural rails for executor handoff — a wedge no incumbent touches (it's adjacent to none of their core businesses, and OpenID is openly calling for standards). Strong top-of-funnel: the executor use case cascades to the whole household.
  • Active-interrogation as the moat. Incumbents do passive ingestion well; none aggressively run Stage-2 interrogation (asking the million questions, requesting subscriptions/accounts) because it feels invasive for them. A trusted neutral party whose entire value prop is the composed picture can earn that permission where a data-monetizing incumbent can't.

Bottom line on white space: the opening is not a better model — it's a neutral, portable, cross-custodian composer with an estate/executor wedge. That is the one position none of the eight leaders can occupy without undermining their own moat.
