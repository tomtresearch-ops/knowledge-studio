Creatine Protocol

Compiled Jun 20, 2026 · KS library (Candow/DOAC, Rhonda Patrick FoundMyFitness, DeLauer, Kelley MD, Azadi, Tim Ferriss) + web research

Best Kind

Creatine monohydrate. Full stop. 30+ years of human research. Cheapest. Most studied. A 2025 RCT confirmed monohydrate and HCl produce identical strength and body composition results in elite athletes. HCl has higher water solubility but no meaningful absorption advantage — the "38x more soluble" claim doesn't translate to better outcomes. Only reason to consider HCl: GI distress with monohydrate, which affects a small minority.

Look for: pure creatine monohydrate, Creapure-certified if you want German-sourced pharmaceutical grade (DeLauer's preferred).

How Much

GoalDoseSource
Muscle / performance / baseline3–5g/dayCandow (120+ papers), ISSN, Azadi
Bone protection (40+)8–12g/dayDr. Darren Candow specifically
Brain / cognitive enhancement10g/dayRhonda Patrick — 10g doubles brain creatine vs 2–4g (neuroimaging confirmed)
Sleep deprivation / acute stress20–25g that dayRhonda Patrick travel protocol; 2022 Scientific Reports study
Rhonda Patrick's actual protocol: 10g daily baseline, bumps to 20–25g when sleep-deprived or traveling. Most evidence-informed stack right now for someone optimizing both body and brain.

Loading phase: Optional. 20g/day × 5–7 days saturates faster. Skip it and take 5g daily — same saturation point in ~3–4 weeks. No performance difference at steady state.

When

Consistency > timing. That's the consistent finding across all sources.

  • On training days: Post-workout slight edge — muscle blood flow is higher, nutrient uptake enhanced.
  • On rest days: Morning with food. Some evidence carbs improve uptake.
  • If taking 10g+: Split doses — 5g AM / 5g PM. Rhonda Patrick's approach. Better GI tolerance, more consistent absorption.
  • Timing note: Creatine absorbs in 1–2 hours, peaks up to 4 hours. For a pre-workout cognitive edge, take 60–90 min before.

Key Signals from Your Library

  • DeLauer [KS 731]: Frames creatine as the underrated longevity compound — cellular energy buffer against mitochondrial decline. Older adults see proportionally bigger benefits than young athletes because cognitive aging is fundamentally an energy problem.
  • Dr. Kelley, ER doc [KS 928]: Flag it to your doctor if taking 10g+. Creatine's metabolite (creatinine) elevates in bloodwork and can falsely suggest kidney damage, triggering unnecessary workups. Kidneys are fine — the number isn't.
  • Alzheimer's pilot (2025): 20g/day × 8 weeks → +11% brain creatine, +17% working memory. Early but significant signal.
  • Vegans / vegetarians: Dramatically larger response — lower dietary baseline means higher ceiling for supplementation benefit.

Bottom Line

Creatine monohydrate, 5g minimum daily, 10g if brain function matters to you. Post-workout or morning, split if 10g+. Don't overthink timing. Consistency is the whole game — it saturates over weeks, not hours.
