## EXTRACTION REQUIREMENTS:

**Comprehensiveness:** Target 1,500-2,000 words. This should be substantial enough to capture all major frameworks, predictions, and insights, but tight enough to remain scannable.

**Structure Balance:** 
- Lead with narrative in Strategic Analysis sections (2-3 flowing paragraphs)
- Use bullets for Factual Details and Predictions (scannable lists)
- Mix quotes throughout Expert Commentary
- This creates rhythm: bullets → quotes → narrative → bullets

**Information Density:**
- Every framework mentioned gets complete breakdown
- Every prediction includes timeline and reasoning
- Every statistic includes context
- Every recommendation includes implementation detail
- No filler or generic statements

**Scannability:**
- Clear visual hierarchy with section numbers and bold headings
- Subsection labels (Factual Details / Expert Commentary / etc.) make it easy to jump to specific types of information
- Mix of bullets and paragraphs prevents wall-of-text fatigue
- Short paragraphs (3-4 sentences max) in Strategic Analysis sections

**Quality Bar:** 
After reading this, someone should understand the expert's complete argument better than if they watched the video. They should be able to scan section headings and subsection labels to quickly find specific types of information (facts vs. analysis vs. predictions).
