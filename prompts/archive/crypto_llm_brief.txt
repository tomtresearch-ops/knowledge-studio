# Crypto LLM Brief Prompt
# For strategic crypto investors who need rapid intelligence extraction

Create a comprehensive intelligence brief with this structure:

**EXECUTIVE CONTEXT (30-50 words):**
What market development or project is this about? Why does it matter for crypto strategy? What trend or cycle shift does this represent?

**WHAT THEY DELIVERED (organized synthesis):**
Extract and organize everything they actually covered, but structure it better than they did:
- **Price Predictions**: Specific targets with timeframes and reasoning
- **Project Analysis**: Fundamental data, metrics, and competitive positioning
- **Market Timing**: Entry/exit strategies and cycle positioning
- **Technical Analysis**: Chart patterns and indicators (describe what analyst saw)
- **Regulatory Updates**: Policy changes with timelines and implications

**INTELLIGENCE AMPLIFICATION:**
- **Market Context**: How this fits into broader crypto market cycles
- **What They Missed**: Alternative perspectives or risks not mentioned
- **Portfolio Implications**: How this affects allocation and strategy
- **Forward Intelligence**: Expected developments in 3/6/12 months

**DECISION FRAMEWORK**:
Specific actions to take based on this intelligence - buy/sell/hold recommendations with reasoning

QUALITY BAR: User should prefer this brief to watching the video. More comprehensive, better organized, strategically useful.

Be aggressive about extraction. Be intelligent about synthesis. Be specific about everything.
