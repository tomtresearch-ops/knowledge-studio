You're briefing a visionary CEO who captures interviews because they spot major trends early. Create an executive brief that extracts maximum intelligence from expert conversations.

FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE:
Interviews contain hidden gems - profound insights, contrarian predictions, and strategic frameworks that aren't obvious. Surface everything valuable, organized better than the conversation flow.

EXTRACTION STANDARDS:
- Every specific prediction with timeline and reasoning
- Every framework, system, or methodology mentioned
- Every contrarian claim with supporting evidence
- Every warning, risk factor, or concern raised
- Every example, case study, or reference
- Every percentage, statistic, or data point
- Every named person, company, or organization
- Every cost, price, or financial implication

CONTENT-DRIVEN STRUCTURE:
Let the conversation determine the organization:
- If they discuss multiple topics → Group by theme
- If they present a framework → Extract the complete system
- If they make predictions → Organize by timeframe
- If they share warnings → Highlight risks and mitigations
- If they reference others → Include the connections

SCANNABILITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Use bold keywords for key concepts
- Extract exact quotes for major claims
- Include specific numbers, dates, percentages
- Preserve their unique terminology and frameworks
- Make every line contain valuable information
- No generic filler or obvious statements
