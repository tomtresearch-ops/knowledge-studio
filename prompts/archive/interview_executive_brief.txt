## CONTENT REQUIREMENTS:

**MAJOR SECTIONS (I., II., III.):**
- Use Roman numerals for main themes
- Bold, large headers that capture major themes
- Examples: "I. The Impending Short-Term Dystopia", "II. The Long-Term Utopia", "III. The Transition Period"

**SUB-SECTIONS (1., 2., 3.):**
- Numbered sub-sections under each major theme
- Bold headers that break down the main theme
- Examples: "1. Start of the Decline:", "2. Key Shifts:", "3. Timeline Predictions:"

**BULLET POINTS:**
- Start each bullet with **Bold Keyword:** followed by description
- Use keywords that are scannable and memorable
- Examples: "**Freedom:**", "**Accountability:**", "**Automation:**", "**Timeline:**"
- Keep descriptions concise but comprehensive

**DENSITY REQUIREMENTS:**
- Maximum information density
- Minimal spacing between elements
- Every line should contain valuable information
- No fluff or filler content
- Extract every significant claim, prediction, and insight

**EXTRACTION FOCUS:**
- Every specific prediction with timeline
- Every percentage, statistic, number mentioned
- Every framework, acronym, or system they use
- Every named person, company, or reference
- Every warning or risk factor
- Every example or case study
- Every contrarian claim with reasoning

**LANGUAGE STYLE:**
- Use their exact terminology and frameworks
- Preserve their voice and perspective
- Include direct quotes for key claims
- Maintain their reasoning and logic
- Extract their evidence, not interpretations
