# Content Distribution Timing Strategy
*Research completed: 2026-02-19*

## Optimal Publishing Schedule

| Asset | Generate | Publish |
|---|---|---|
| Brief | 9:30 PM | Website immediately |
| Podcast | 9:45 PM | 4:00-5:00 AM next day |
| Newsletter | 10:00 PM | 6:00-6:30 AM next day |
| YouTube video | Overnight render | 5:00-6:00 AM next day |

## Key Findings

### When Major AI/Tech Announcements Drop
- **10:00 AM PT / 1:00 PM ET** is the industry standard (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Apple)
- Tuesday through Thursday dominate for major launches
- Weekends are dead for announcements

### Newsletter Timing (Competitive Landscape)
- TLDR: 6:00 AM ET (5M+ subs across verticals)
- The Rundown AI: ~8:00 AM ET (2M+ subs)
- The Neuron: 8:00 AM ET (600K+ subs)
- 6-8 AM window is about **inbox position** — top of stack when people open email
- These newsletters cover yesterday's news, creating a **17-19 hour gap** after announcements
- Our 6:00-6:30 AM send time lands concurrent with or ahead of the majors

### YouTube: Freshness & Algorithm Mechanics
- **First-hour velocity is critical** — YouTube tracks views, likes, comments in the first 60 minutes
- **First 48-72 hours** determine the video's trajectory
- Publishing **2-3 hours before peak audience** delivers **20-35% boost** in first-48-hour performance
- For topical/news content, YouTube heavily weights recency in search results
- YouTube content half-life: **9.67 days** (vs Twitter 49 min, Reddit 2.58 hrs)

### Why 5-6 AM ET for YouTube
- At 5 AM ET: India is at 3:30 PM (peak), UK at 10 AM (active), Central Europe at 11 AM (active)
- International viewers serve as **seed audience** — their engagement gives the algorithm data
- By 7-8 AM ET when US wakes up, the video has 1-2 hours of real engagement data
- "Posted 2 hours ago" reads fresh for news content
- 80% of YouTube views come from outside the US — international audience is not a bonus, it's the majority
- 2 AM ET is too early (dead zone, insufficient signal for algorithm)
- 7 AM ET is too late (competing with every other US creator, zero pre-built momentum)

### Podcast Timing
- Peak listening shifted to **10 AM-2 PM** (home listening surpassed commute)
- 67% of podcast listening now happens at home, not in cars
- **4:00-5:00 AM ET** publish puts episodes in feeds before the morning surge
- Tuesday and Wednesday see strongest download performance

### AI/Tech News Cycle Pattern
- Hour 0 (1 PM ET): Announcement drops
- Hours 0-2: Twitter/X explosion, HN submission, hot takes
- Hours 2-6: Twitter peaks and decays, HN peaks, Reddit threads form
- Hours 6-12: Second wave — analysis pieces, YouTube reactions
- Hours 12-20: HN falls off, newsletter writers composing
- Hours 17-20 (next morning 6-8 AM ET): Newsletters hit inboxes
- Day 2-3: YouTube long-form analysis peaks, LinkedIn peaks
- Day 3-7: Podcast deep-dives land

## Strategic Implications

### Current Position
The 9:30 PM synthesis captures the full day's signal set. No quality sacrifice needed. The overnight pipeline naturally positions us to distribute before the morning newsletters — we're covering today's news while they're still composing.

### Future Product: Afternoon Topical Piece
- Not a news flash — ~8 min synthesis/perspective on the day's biggest story
- Published late afternoon for evening US YouTube browsing
- Brand positioning: "synthesis and perspective, not dropping news"
- Design later, after video pipeline is built
